The rental rack has been rebuilt. Twelve new frames, replacing the tired ones that
had been passed around all summer, and this time they are sorted rather than
piled together.

## Three shelves, three weights

- **Light (355–365 g), round shape.** Forgiving through the middle, easy on the
  wrist. Start here if you are new, or coming over from tennis.
- **Medium (365–370 g), teardrop.** The one most regulars end up on — enough
  control to place a ball, enough head weight to finish a point.
- **Heavy (370–375 g), diamond.** Power at the net, and it punishes a late
  swing. Take it only if you already know you want it.

Ask at reception for a shelf rather than a racket, and try the next one up or
down mid-session if it doesn't suit. Nobody minds a swap.

## What it costs

Rental stays at ₾8 per racket, and you can add it to a booking online at the
extras step instead of sorting it out at the desk. Balls are ₾5 for a can of
three.

If you play often enough that the rack has started to annoy you, that is usually
the sign it is time to buy your own — bring what you are considering and hit
with it here first.
