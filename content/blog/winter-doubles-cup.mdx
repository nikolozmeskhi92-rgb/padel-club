The Autumn League runs eight weeks, which is the right shape for people who can
commit to a fixed evening. It is the wrong shape for everyone else. So the plan
for December is the opposite: one weekend, start to finish.

## The format, as it stands

- **16 pairs**, one weekend, Saturday groups and Sunday knockouts
- Groups of four, so **every pair plays at least three matches** — nobody drives
  across town to lose once and go home
- Matches to one set with a tie-break at 5–5, which keeps a court to about 45
  minutes and the schedule honest
- Two levels running side by side, so a first tournament is not a first beating

Courts 1 and 2 stay open for normal bookings through the weekend. The rest go to
the cup.

## What we haven't settled

The entry fee, and whether to seed the groups or draw them blind. Seeding makes
the Sunday better and the Saturday duller; a blind draw does the reverse. We
lean towards seeding the top level and drawing the other one.

Tell reception what you think, or say it to whoever you are playing with next
week and let it get back to us — that is how the mixed division ended up in the
Autumn League.

Registration opens once the dates are fixed. If you want to be told directly
rather than finding out from this page, leave your name at the desk.
