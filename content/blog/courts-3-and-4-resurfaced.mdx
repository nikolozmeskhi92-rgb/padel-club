Courts 3 and 4 have new turf. Four days of work, both back in the booking grid,
and the difference is obvious the moment you play on them — the old surface had
gone shiny down the middle where four years of feet had flattened it.

## It will play slow at first

New turf is filled with fresh silica sand, and the sand takes a couple of weeks
of play to settle into the pile. Until it does:

- the ball **grips and sits up** rather than skidding through
- your usual approach lands shorter than you expect
- the surface sheds a little sand — that is normal and it stops

If you are chasing a result, book 5 to 10 in the meantime. If you want to work on
your volleys, 3 and 4 are the friendliest courts in the club right now.

## The rest of the schedule

The remaining outdoor courts are on the same rotation and will be done a pair at
a time, out of season, never more than two closed at once. We will say here which
pair is next before it happens, so nobody arrives to find their court missing.
