Registration for the Autumn League is open. Eight weeks of round-robin play across
four divisions, culminating in a finals night on Court 1 with live scoring on the
big screen.

## What's new this season

- Mixed doubles division added alongside Men's, Women's, and Open
- Matches scheduled automatically around your existing court bookings
- Live standings published weekly on this page

Spots are limited to 32 pairs per division. Book a court today and ask reception
about league sign-up, or reserve your spot online once registration opens fully
next week.
